Common questions

What advisors, CPAs, and tax preparers usually ask before offering HSA Monster to their clients.

Can I offer HSA Monster to my clients?

Yes. We provide a batch of redemption codes for your practice, and you distribute them to your clients however you already reach them — your portal, email, or in a meeting. There's no minimum technical setup on your end.

How does billing work for firms?

We invoice you directly — no App Store account or per-client checkout required. Licensing is priced in annual blocks based on how many clients you'd like to offer it to, with discounts that grow as your block size grows. Tell us roughly how many clients and we'll send a quote.

How long does a license last, and how do renewals work?

Client licenses run for one year. Before your block expires, we'll reach out to renew it directly with you, so your clients keep uninterrupted access. Renewals are handled through the same direct invoice so your clients are never charged individually.

Do my clients need to create an account?

No. HSA Monster has no accounts and no logins. Clients redeem a code, install the app, and their data lives privately in their own iCloud. Nothing for you — or us — to administer per client.

Is my clients' financial data private?

Yes. All AI receipt scanning runs on-device, and there are no third-party servers. Receipts and data never leave the client's device or their personal iCloud, which keeps it simple from a privacy and compliance standpoint.

Can I try it myself before recommending it?

Yes. Verified financial advisors, CPAs, and tax preparers get a free year for their own personal use — separate from client licensing — so you can evaluate the app before offering it to clients. Just let us know in the form below.

What's the minimum to set up a client block?

The entry block covers up to 10 clients, which keeps the program manageable to administer. If you have fewer than that, clients can subscribe directly in the app.
