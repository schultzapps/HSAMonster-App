Your HSA can pay for your spouse's and dependents' medical expenses too. Add family members to keep track of who each expense was for.

## Adding a family member

1. Go to **Settings → Family Members → Add Family Member**.
2. Enter a **Name** and pick a **Color**.
3. Tap **Save**.

You can also tap **Add** next to **Patient** while adding an expense.

## Using family members

- Pick a **Patient** on each expense.
- When a scanned receipt includes a patient name that matches a family member, they're selected for you.
- Search a name to see that person's expenses.
- Tap a family member in **Settings → Family Members** for their number of transactions and **Total Expenses**.

## Deleting a family member

Tap the family member, then **Edit → Delete Family Member**. Their expenses are kept, with no patient assigned.
