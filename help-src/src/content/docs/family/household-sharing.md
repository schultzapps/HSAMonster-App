Share your HSA Monster data with your spouse or partner using iCloud. You both see every transaction and receipt, and either of you can scan and add expenses. We recommend creating your household as soon as possible after becoming a user, and limiting changes after. Repeatadly leaving and joining households could cause syncing problems. If you need to revise your sharing settings, export a copy of your transactions and receipts first.

## Sharing with your partner

1. Go to **Settings → Household Sharing → Create Household**.
2. Enter a **Household Name**, like "Smith Family," and tap **Create**.
3. Choose how to send the invitation, like Messages or Mail.

All your existing transactions, accounts, and family members are added to the household.

Both of you need to be signed in to iCloud. You can share with one household at a time.

## Joining a household

1. Open the invitation on your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.
2. HSA Monster opens. Tap **Join Household**.

If you're already in a household, leave it first.

## Managing the household

In **Settings → Household Sharing**:

- **Edit** renames the household (owner only).
- **Manage** adds or removes people (owner only).
- **View Members** shows who's in it.
- **••• → Resync with Household** fetches the latest if something's missing.

## Stopping sharing

- **Owner:** **••• → Remove Household**. Your partner loses access, and you keep all your data.
- **Partner:** **••• → Leave Household**. You lose access to the household's data.

## FAQ

### Does my partner need their own subscription?

No, not with the **Yearly** plan. It's shared through Apple Family Sharing, so your partner gets it too:

1. Make sure you're both in the same Apple Family. On iPhone, open the **Settings** app, tap your name, then **Family**.
2. On the subscriber's iPhone, open the **Settings** app, tap your name, then **Subscriptions → HSA Monster**, and make sure **Share With Family** is on.
3. On your partner's device, open HSA Monster and tap **Settings → Restore Access**, or **Restore** on the upgrade screen.

### Why doesn't a new receipt show up on my partner's device yet?

On a normal internet connection, receipts should duplicate to another device within a few seconds. Sometimes iCloud takes a bit longer, so be patient while your data is uploaded and synced.
