HSA Monster saves your expenses, receipts, and accounts in your private iCloud account. They sync automatically to every iPhone, iPad, and Mac signed in to the same Apple Account. There's nothing to turn on and no sign-up.

## Checking sync

Go to **Settings → iCloud Sync**:

- **Network:** whether you're online
- **Sync Status:** **Up to date**, **Syncing...**, **No network**, **Not signed in**, or an error
- **Last Synced:** when it last finished
- **Records:** how many transactions, receipts, family members, and accounts are on this device

## Setting up a new device

1. Sign in to the same Apple Account and install HSA Monster.
2. On the first welcome screen, tap **Returning User**.
3. When you see **iCloud Data Found**, tap **Open HSA Monster**.

Your purchase is restored automatically with the same Apple Account.

## If data is missing

1. Check **Settings → iCloud Sync**. **Not signed in** or **No network** tells you what to fix.
2. In the iPhone **Settings** app, tap your name, then **iCloud**, and make sure iCloud is on for **HSA Monster**.
3. Allow a few minutes, especially with many receipts. Keep the app open on Wi-Fi.
4. If you share a household, tap **Settings → Household Sharing → ••• → Resync with Household**.

If you still need help, tap **Settings → Contact Us → Report a Problem**.
