Go to **Settings → Reports & Export**. Each button creates the file and opens the share sheet, so you can save it to Files, email it, or AirDrop it.

## Reimbursement report

Proof of what you've spent out of pocket and reimbursed, for your records, your tax preparer, or an IRS request.

1. Choose a **Period**: **All Time** or a single year.
2. Next to **Statement**, tap **PDF** or **CSV**.

The report lists every out-of-pocket expense and reimbursement with its provider, patient, and receipt status. Reimbursements are applied to your oldest expenses first, so each expense shows as **Fully Reimbursed**, **Partially Reimbursed**, or **Not Reimbursed**, with a running balance.

For a single year, the PDF summary shows your starting balance, new expenses, reimbursements taken, and ending balance.

## Receipts ZIP

Next to **Receipts**, tap **ZIP** for every receipt in the period, organized into year and month folders. File names start with the date, provider, and amount.

## All transactions

Under **All Transactions**, choose a **Date Range** and tap **CSV** next to **Export Data**. It includes every expense, contribution, reimbursement, and reconciliation, and opens in Numbers, Excel, or Google Sheets.

## Exporting a selection

To export just some transactions or receipts, select them on **Expenses** or **Search** and use **Export Transactions** or **Export Receipts**. See [Search & batch edit](/help/expenses/search-and-batch-edit/).

:::tip
Export a receipts ZIP and an All Time report once a year and store them somewhere safe, like iCloud Drive or a backup drive.
:::
