Some transactions happen on a schedule: a weekly therapy session, a monthly prescription, the contribution that comes out of every paycheck. Set them up once and HSA Monster adds each one on its date.

## Setting up a recurring transaction

1. Start a new expense, contribution, or reimbursement as usual.
2. Under **Date**, tap **Repeat** and choose how often:
   - **Weekly**, **Every other week**, **Twice a month**, or **Every 4 weeks**
   - **Monthly**, **Every other month**, **Every 3 months**, or **Every 4 months**
   - **Yearly**
3. Tap **Save**.

If the date is today or earlier, the first transaction is added right away, and the next one is scheduled from today on. Dates in between aren't filled in.

A series started on the 31st lands on the last day of shorter months, then goes back to the 31st.

You can also start a series from a transaction you've already added: edit it and pick a **Repeat**.

Reconciliations can't repeat.

## Adding a future transaction

Pick a date after today, with or without **Repeat**. It waits in **Scheduled** until its day, then it's added.

Documents can't be attached until the transaction is added, so the **Document** row is hidden for a future date.

## The Scheduled section

Upcoming transactions are listed in a **Scheduled** section at the top of **Summary**, **Expenses**, **Search**, and each account's page. Each one shows its next date. Tap the header to collapse or expand it.

- **Tap** a scheduled transaction to change it. Changes apply to the rest of the series.
- **Swipe** or **press and hold** for **Edit** and **Delete**.
- On **Expenses**, the section follows the list's **Sort By**. On **Summary**, the soonest is first.
- On **Search**, tap the **Scheduled** suggestion to list only scheduled transactions.
- In **Select**, scheduled transactions can be selected with the rest for **Delete** or **Combine Provider**.

## Stopping a series

Delete the scheduled transaction. The series stops, and everything it already added stays. A scheduled transaction isn't moved to the Trash, since no money has moved yet.

## When transactions are added

Due transactions are added when you open HSA Monster, or when the day changes while it's open. If you haven't opened it in a while, every missed date is added at once.

If you [share a household](/help/family/household-sharing/), any device in it can add them, and each one is only added once.

If your subscription ends, your series are kept but nothing new is added, and a note under **Scheduled** explains why. Subscribe again to pick back up.

## Reviewing new transactions

Transactions added from a schedule arrive unreviewed, marked with a colored bar in your lists. A **New Transactions** row with a **Review** button shows how many, at the top of **Summary**'s **Recent Activity**, the **Expenses** list, and an account's page.

1. Tap the row to open the review.
2. Tap the circles to check the ones that look right, then tap **Approve**.
3. To add a receipt or change the amount first, tap a transaction. It opens with **Approve** in place of **Save**.

Approved one by mistake? Tap **Undo**. You can also swipe an unreviewed transaction anywhere to **Approve** it, or approve several from **Select** on **Expenses** or **Search**.

:::tip
For a monthly prescription, set the expense to repeat **Monthly**. When it's added, open it from the review and scan that month's receipt.
:::
