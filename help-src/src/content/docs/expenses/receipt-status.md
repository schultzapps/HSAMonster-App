Every expense has a receipt status. The **Expenses** tab badge counts the ones still **Missing**.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Attached** | The receipt is saved in HSA Monster. |
| **Missing** | You still need to add it. New expenses start here until you attach one. |
| **Not Required** | No receipt needed for this one. |
| **Stored Elsewhere** | You keep it somewhere else, like your HSA provider's site. |
| **Lost** | It's gone for good. |

## Setting a status

- **One expense:** edit it, tap **•••** next to **Document**, and pick a status.
- **Several at once:** select them and use **Receipt Status**. See [Search & batch edit](/help/expenses/search-and-batch-edit/).

## Finding expenses without receipts

On **Expenses**, tap the **filter** button and choose **Receipt Status → Missing**. Or search for "missing."

## Only counting expenses with receipts

The IRS can ask for proof of any reimbursement. Turn on **Settings → Exclude Missing Receipts** and expenses marked **Missing** or **Lost** are left out of your Reimbursable total and reports.

## Weekly reminders

Turn on **Settings → Reminders** for a weekly nudge to add attachments to recent transactions without one.

The first time, iOS asks to allow notifications. If you declined before, the switch opens the iOS settings so you can allow them.
