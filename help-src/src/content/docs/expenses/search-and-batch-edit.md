## Searching

Open **Search** and type anything:

- A provider, description, comment, or family member
- An amount, like **42.10** or **$42.10**
- A receipt status, like **missing** or **lost**
- A type, like **contribution** or **reimbursement**

Every word must match, so "cvs jane" finds CVS expenses for Jane. Tap a **Suggestion** to search one specific field, like a provider or person.

## Sorting and filtering

On **Expenses**, tap the **filter** button:

- **Receipt Status:** show only one status, like **Missing**
- **Sort By:** **Newest First**, **Oldest First**, **Recently Added**, **Recently Updated**, **Highest Amount**, **Lowest Amount**, or **Provider A-Z**

Date sorts group expenses by month. Tap **Clear Filters** to reset.

## Quick actions

- **Swipe right:** **Add Receipt** or **View Receipt**
- **Swipe left:** **Edit** or **Delete**
- **Press and hold:** all of the above, plus **Reimburse**. On an expense that already has a receipt, you'll also see **Add Document**, and **View Documents** when it has more than one.

## Selecting several at once

1. On **Expenses**, or **Search** with results, tap **Select**.
2. Tap transactions to select them, or **Select All**. The bottom bar shows how many and their total.
3. Tap <span class="help-icon help-icon--dots-bold" role="img" aria-label="More Actions"></span> and choose an action:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Receipt Status** | Set the status for expenses without receipts. |
| **Combine Provider** | Give every selected expense the same provider and rename their receipts and other documents to match. Useful for merging "CVS" and "CVS Pharmacy." |
| **Export Transactions** | Share a CSV of just the selection. |
| **Export Receipts** | Share a ZIP of their receipts, when every one has a receipt. |
| **Delete** | Move them to the [Trash](/help/expenses/trash/). |

:::tip
Search first, then select. For example, search a provider and year, **Select All**, and export just those receipts.
:::
