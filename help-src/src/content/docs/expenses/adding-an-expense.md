## Adding an expense

1. Tap <span class="help-icon help-icon--plus" role="img" aria-label="Add"></span> on **Summary** or **Expenses** (Mac: **⇧ ⌘ N**).
2. Choose how to start:
   - **Scan Document**, **Attach Photo**, or **Attach File** reads the receipt and fills in the details for you. See [Scanning receipts](/help/expenses/scanning-receipts/).
   - **Create Manual Expense** starts with a blank form.
3. Check the details, then tap **Save**.

## The fields

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Document** | The receipt. Tap **Add** to scan or attach one, or use <span class="help-icon help-icon--dots-circle" role="img" aria-label="More"></span> to mark it **Not Required**, **Stored Elsewhere**, or **Lost**. You can attach more than one, like an EOB. See [Adding more than one document](/help/expenses/multiple-documents/). |
| **Date** | When you paid. You can pick a future date and it's added on that day. |
| **Repeat** | How often it happens, like **Monthly**, for a regular prescription or copay. See [Recurring & future transactions](/help/expenses/recurring-transactions/). |
| **Amount** | Type the digits; the decimal fills in automatically. |
| **Payment** | **Out of Pocket** if you paid with your own money, or the HSA account you paid from. |
| **Provider** | Who you paid, like a doctor's office or pharmacy. Required. |
| **Description** | What it was for, like "Annual physical." |
| **Patient** | The [family member](/help/family/family-members/) it was for. |
| **Comment** | Any notes. |

## Out of Pocket vs. paid from HSA

This choice decides where the expense counts:

- **Out of Pocket** adds it to your **Reimbursable** total, the money you can pay yourself back later.
- **An HSA account** subtracts it from that account's balance instead. You can't reimburse yourself for it, since your HSA already paid.

## Providers

Tap **Provider** to pick one you've used before or type a new name and tap **Create**. Reusing the same name keeps search, reports, and receipt file names consistent.

To merge providers that were typed differently, select their expenses and use **Combine Provider**. See [Search & batch edit](/help/expenses/search-and-batch-edit/).

## Editing an expense

Tap an expense to see its details, then tap **Edit**. Changing the date, provider, or amount also renames the expense's documents to match.
