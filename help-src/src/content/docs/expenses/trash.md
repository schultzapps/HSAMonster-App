## Deleting a transaction

Swipe left on it and tap **Delete**, or edit it and tap **Delete Transaction**. It moves to the Trash, along with its receipt.

## Restoring from the Trash

1. Go to **Settings → Trash**.
2. Swipe right on a transaction and tap **Restore**.

Items stay in the Trash for 30 days, then they're permanently deleted.

## Deleting permanently

- **One item:** swipe left in the Trash and tap **Delete**. This happens right away, with no confirmation.
- **Everything:** tap **Empty**, then **Delete All Permanently**.

Permanently deleted transactions and receipts are removed from iCloud and every device. This can't be undone.
