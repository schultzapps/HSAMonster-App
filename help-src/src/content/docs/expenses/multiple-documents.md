Some HSA expenses require more than one document to fully support your claim. Attach receipts, itemized bills, insurance explanations of benefits (EOBs), or other supporting records to a single transaction to keep everything organized in one place.

## Adding another document

1. Tap an expense, then tap **Edit**.
2. Tap the add button <span class="help-icon help-icon--add-document" role="img" aria-label="Add"></span> (on iOS 18 it's labeled **Add**), and choose **Scan Document**, **Attach Photo**, or **Attach File**. In Files you can pick several files at once.
3. Tap **Save**.

Nothing is saved until you tap **Save**. If you cancel after adding or removing documents, HSA Monster asks if you want to **Discard** them.

On an expense that already has a receipt, you can also press and hold it and tap **Add Document**. That works on **Summary**, **Expenses**, **Search**, and an account's page.

## Looking through your documents

- **In the form:** the receipt card pages sideways between documents, with dots beneath it when there's more than one. On Mac, use the arrows on either side of the dots.
- **On the expense:** the first document you attached stays as the header picture and as the thumbnail in lists, so adding an EOB never replaces the receipt. When there's more than one, a paperclip shows how many.
- **In the viewer:** tap the header, or press and hold the expense and tap **View Documents**. The bottom bar shows where you are, like **2 of 3**, with arrows to step through. Tap **Share** to send the one showing. On Mac, Quick Look steps through all of them.

## Removing a document

In the form, use the menu on that document and choose **Remove Document**, then tap **Save**. In the viewer, tap the remove button and choose **Remove Document**. The others stay.

## File names

An expense's documents share one name made from the date, provider, and amount, numbered in the order you attached them:

- `2026-10-15_Costco-Optical_249.90.pdf`
- `2026-10-15_Costco-Optical_249.90_2.pdf`
- `2026-10-15_Costco-Optical_249.90_3.pdf`

Changing the date, provider, or amount renames all of them to match, and so does **Combine Provider**. Removing one renumbers the rest.

## Exports and the Trash

- A [receipts ZIP](/help/data/reports-and-export/) keeps each expense's documents together, filed under the expense's date, so an EOB that arrives months later sits beside its receipt.
- Deleting an expense moves all of its documents to the [Trash](/help/expenses/trash/) with it.

:::tip
Attach the EOB to the original expense as soon as it arrives. At tax time, one search gets you the whole paper trail.
:::
