## From another app

If you have a receipt in another app, like Mail, Messages, Safari, or your patient portal, you can add it directly:

1. Tap **Share** on the image or PDF.
2. Tap **Add to HSA Monster** in the list of actions, or the **HSA Monster** icon.

HSA Monster opens, reads the receipt, and fills in a new expense for you to check and save. This works with one image or PDF at a time, including screenshots.

## Siri

Say any of these:

| To | Say |
| --- | --- |
| Scan a receipt | "Scan a receipt in HSA Monster" |
| Add an expense | "Add an expense in HSA Monster" |
| Log a reimbursement | "Log a reimbursement in HSA Monster" |

See every phrase in **Settings → Siri Shortcuts**. The same actions are in the **Shortcuts** app for your own automations, and you can assign one to the **Action Button**.

## Control Center

1. Open **Control Center** and tap **+** at the top left.
2. Tap **Add a Control** and search for **HSA Monster**.
3. Choose **Scan Receipt**, **Add Expense**, or **Reimburse Myself**.

Controls can also go on the Lock Screen.
