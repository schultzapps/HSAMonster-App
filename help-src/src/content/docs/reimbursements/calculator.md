Go to **Settings → Calculator**. Switch between two modes at the top:

| Mode | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Invest** | How your HSA could grow with yearly contributions, compared with a taxable brokerage account. |
| **Defer** | How much more your Reimbursable total could be worth if you leave it invested instead of reimbursing now. |

Drag across the chart to see any year. Results are in today's dollars, adjusted for inflation.

## Invest

- **Starting HSA balance:** starts at your current balance
- **Coverage:** **Individual** or **Family**
- **Annual contribution:** up to this year's IRS maximum
- **Federal bracket**, **State tax**, and **Payroll (pre-tax) contribution** (avoids 7.65% FICA tax)

**Total Tax Saved** adds up the taxes you avoid on contributions. **vs Taxable Brokerage** shows how far ahead the HSA ends up.

## Defer

**Unreimbursed expenses** starts at your current Reimbursable total.

**Reimbursable Now** is what you could take today. **Growth Forfeited** is the growth you'd give up by taking it now instead of letting it compound.

## Both modes

Adjust **Years invested**, **Annual return**, and **Inflation rate**. Your inputs are remembered. Tap the reset arrow to go back to your live balance.

Tap **Share** to send HSA Monster to a friend.
