When you withdraw money from your HSA to pay yourself back, log it in HSA Monster so your Reimbursable total stays accurate.

## Reimbursing a specific expense

1. Press and hold an out-of-pocket expense on **Summary**, **Expenses**, or **Search**.
2. Tap **Reimburse**.
3. Choose the **Account** the money came from.
4. Check the amount and date, then tap **Save**.

The amount and a comment describing the expense are filled in for you.

## Logging any reimbursement

To reimburse several expenses at once, or any amount:

- Press and hold an account on **Summary** and tap **Add Reimbursement**.
- On an account's page, tap <span class="help-icon help-icon--plus" role="img" aria-label="Add"></span> → **Add Reimbursement**.
- Say "Log a reimbursement in HSA Monster," or use the **Reimburse Myself** control in Control Center.

If you pay yourself back on a regular schedule, set **Repeat** on the reimbursement. See [Recurring & future transactions](/help/expenses/recurring-transactions/).

## How it's counted

A reimbursement lowers both your **Reimbursable** total and that account's **HSA Balance**.

Your [reimbursement report](/help/data/reports-and-export/) applies reimbursements to your oldest expenses first and shows each expense as **Fully Reimbursed**, **Partially Reimbursed**, or **Not Reimbursed**.

:::tip
Before withdrawing, switch the Summary chart to **Reimbursable** to see how much you can claim.
:::
