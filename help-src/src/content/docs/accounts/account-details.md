Tap an account on **Summary** or in **Settings → HSA Accounts**. On iPad and Mac, accounts are also in the sidebar.

## What's there

- **Balance chart** and **YTD Contributions** and **YTD Withdrawals** for just this account
- **Activity:** its transactions in the selected date range
- **+:** **Add Contribution**, **Add Reimbursement**, and **Reconcile**

Use the **filter** button to show one **Transaction Type** or change **Sort By**.

## Account Info

Tap **Info** for all-time totals: **Total Contributions**, **Total Withdrawals**, **Total Paid from HSA**, and the number of transactions.

## Editing an account

Tap **Edit** to change the institution, name, or account number.

## Archiving an account

Moved your HSA to a new provider? Archive the old account to keep its history without cluttering Summary.

1. Bring the balance to $0, for example by reconciling to $0 after the transfer.
2. Tap **Edit → Archive Account**.

Archived accounts stay in **Settings → HSA Accounts**, where you can unarchive them anytime.

## Deleting an account

**Edit → Delete Account** permanently deletes the account and all of its transactions and receipts. They don't go to the Trash, and this can't be undone. Archive instead if you want to keep the history.
