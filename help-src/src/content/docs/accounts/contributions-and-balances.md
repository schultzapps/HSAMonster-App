An account's balance is built from its transactions:

| Transaction | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Contribution | Adds money |
| Reimbursement | Takes money out |
| Expense paid from the HSA | Takes money out |
| Reconciliation | Adjusts to match your statement |

## Adding a contribution

1. Press and hold the account on **Summary**, or open it and tap **+**.
2. Tap **Add Contribution**.
3. Enter the amount and date, then tap **Save**.

Log each paycheck deposit, or one total per month or year. Contributions add up in **YTD Contributions** on Summary.

## Reconciling your balance

HSA Monster builds your balance from what you log. It doesn't connect to your HSA provider, so anything else that changes your balance needs a reconcile to catch up.

That matters most if your HSA is invested. Market gains and losses, dividends, interest, and fees all change your balance without a transaction you'd log. Reconciling also records your investment growth, so it shows up on your [chart](#seeing-your-growth).

To bring HSA Monster in line with your provider:

1. Press and hold the account and tap **Reconcile**.
2. Choose how to enter it:
   - **Set:** type the **Balance** from your statement. The **Adjustment** is calculated for you.
   - **Increase By** or **Decrease By:** enter just the change.
3. Tap **Save**.

Reconcile only after you've logged all contributions and withdrawals up to that date. Otherwise the reconcile adjustment absorbs them, and they'll be counted twice once you add them.

Reconciling monthly or quarterly, when your statement arrives, is plenty.

## Seeing your growth

On **Summary**, switch the chart to **HSA Balance**. The dotted line is what you've put in, minus what you've taken out. The space between it and your balance is your investment growth.
