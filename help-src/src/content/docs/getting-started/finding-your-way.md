## Tabs

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Summary** | Your reimbursable total and HSA balance, accounts, and recent activity. See [The Summary screen](/help/getting-started/summary/). |
| **Expenses** | Every expense, newest first. The badge counts expenses still missing a receipt. |
| **Search** | Find any transaction by provider, person, amount, and more. See [Search & batch edit](/help/expenses/search-and-batch-edit/). |

On iOS 18, Search is the magnifying glass at the top of **Expenses**.

## Settings

- **iPhone:** tap the **gear** at the top right of **Summary**.
- **iPad and Mac:** tap **Settings** in the sidebar. The sidebar also lists each HSA account with its balance.

Settings is where you manage **HSA Accounts**, **Family Members**, **Reports & Export**, **Import Data**, and the **Trash**.

## Adding things quickly

Tap **+** on **Summary** or **Expenses**:

- **Scan Document**
- **Attach Photo**
- **Attach File**
- **Create Manual Expense**

You can also add receipts from the share sheet, Siri, or Control Center. See [Share sheet, Siri & Control Center](/help/expenses/shortcuts/).

Scanning with the camera isn't available on Mac. Use **Attach File** instead.

## Simple Mode

To track only out-of-pocket expenses, turn on **Settings → Simple Mode**. It hides HSA account balances, contributions, and activity, and the Summary chart shows only your **Reimbursable** total.

## Make it yours

In **Settings**:

- **Accent Color:** Indigo, Blue, Orange, or Teal
- **Appearance:** Default (follows your device), Light, or Dark
- **Smooth Charts:** curved or straight chart lines
