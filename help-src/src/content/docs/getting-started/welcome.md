HSA Monster keeps every HSA-eligible expense and receipt in one place, so you can let your HSA grow and reimburse yourself tax-free whenever you choose.

## The strategy

1. **Invest every dollar.** Contribute to your HSA and invest it so it grows tax-free.
2. **Pay out of pocket.** Pay medical bills with your own money and save the receipt in HSA Monster.
3. **Reimburse yourself anytime.** Withdraw tax-free whenever you need it, even years later. HSA Monster tracks every dollar you can claim.

See [How reimbursing later works](/help/reimbursements/reimburse-later/) for more.

## Setting up

1. [Add an expense](/help/expenses/adding-an-expense/), ideally by scanning a receipt.
2. [Add your HSA account](/help/accounts/adding-an-account/) to track its balance and contributions (optional).
3. [Add family members](/help/family/family-members/) to note who each expense was for.

## Your data

Everything is saved in your private iCloud account, not on our servers. There's no sign-up, and it syncs to every iPhone, iPad, and Mac signed in to the same iCloud account.

Receipts are read on your device by default. See [Privacy & your data](/help/data/privacy/).

## New device or reinstall

On the first welcome screen, tap **Returning User**. HSA Monster waits for your data to arrive from iCloud, then shows **iCloud Data Found**. Tap **Open HSA Monster**. Everything can take a few minutes to finish syncing.

See [iCloud sync](/help/data/icloud-sync/) if something doesn't show up.
