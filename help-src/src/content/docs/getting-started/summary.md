## The chart

The chart at the top tracks one of two totals. Tap the label under the amount to switch:

| Total | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Reimbursable** | Out-of-pocket expenses minus everything you've reimbursed: what you can still withdraw tax-free. |
| **HSA Balance** | The combined balance of your HSA accounts. |

In **HSA Balance**, a dotted line shows **net contributed**: contributions minus withdrawals. The gap between the two lines is your investment growth.

Drag across the chart to see the value on any date. The big number always shows today's total.

:::tip
Turn on **Settings → Exclude Missing Receipts** to leave out expenses marked **Missing** or **Lost** from your Reimbursable total, so it only counts what you can prove.
:::

## Date range

Pick **3M**, **6M**, **YTD**, **1Y**, **3Y**, **5Y**, or **ALL**, or a specific year. The range also applies to the stats below and to each account's page.

## Contributions and withdrawals

**YTD Contributions** and **YTD Withdrawals** total the current year, or the year you picked. Withdrawals include reimbursements and expenses paid from your HSA.

Tap either one for a year-by-year **History** with all-time totals.

## Accounts

Your HSA accounts, highest balance first. Tap one to open it, or press and hold for **Add Contribution**, **Add Reimbursement**, and **Reconcile**. **See All** opens **HSA Accounts**.

## Scheduled

Transactions waiting for a future date, soonest first. Tap the header to collapse it. See [Recurring & future transactions](/help/expenses/recurring-transactions/).

## Recent Activity

Everything from the last 30 days. Tap an expense to see its details, or press and hold for more actions.

When scheduled transactions have been added, a **New Transactions** row at the top shows how many. Tap it to [review them](/help/expenses/recurring-transactions/#reviewing-new-transactions).
