Changelog

What's New?

Updates, improvements, and new features in HSA Monster.

Version 1.1.3

October 12, 2026
  • Select lots of transactions at once, then re-label, combine, export, or delete them in one go
  • Reimburse an expense with a long-press. We fill in the details for you
  • A friendly Thursday nudge if receipts are still missing (optional, in Settings)
  • Smarter search: suggestions come from your own data, multi-word searches just work, and results get swipe actions
  • Big photos now attach without a fuss, and if something can't, we'll tell you why
  • Now works smoothly on iPhone Duo, adapting as you fold and unfold
  • Indigo looks better in dark mode
  • General polish and stability improvements

Version 1.1.2

September 30, 2026
  • Scanned receipts and PDFs now look sharp everywhere, not just right after scanning
  • Reports and exports share in a single tap, and on Mac you can save them straight to a folder
  • Bug fixes and performance improvements

Version 1.1.1

September 19, 2026
  • Attaching a document to an expense now takes a single row on the transaction form — scan it, pick a photo or a file, or mark that a receipt isn't needed
  • Refreshed buttons and status icons throughout, so a receipt's state reads at a glance
  • General polish and stability improvements

Version 1.1

September 18, 2026
  • Now supporting Private Cloud Compute, Apple's privacy-focused cloud AI. Opt in at Settings > Document Scanning, since it's the only setting where data leaves your device
  • Rebuilt receipt scanning for both on-device Apple Intelligence and Private Cloud Compute, with better accuracy across every kind of receipt
  • After each scan, Apple Intelligence notes what it filled in and flags anything worth a second look before you save
  • Crisper, clearer document thumbnails — tap one to open a document preview
  • Amounts now format into dollars automatically as you type
  • Picking a provider opens a searchable list of everyone you've used, so you don't end up with three spellings of the same doctor's office
  • A new What's New screen in Settings keeps you up to date on each release
  • Plus general polish and stability improvements throughout

Version 1.0.7

July 27, 2026
  • New HSA Investment Calculator — project how far your HSA can grow tax-free over time, with a live chart comparing it against a regular taxable brokerage account and your total tax saved
  • New "Deferred Reimbursement" mode shows the payoff of paying a medical bill out of pocket today and reimbursing yourself years down the road, after your money has grown
  • General polish and stability improvements

Version 1.0.5

June 18, 2026
  • Some work under the hood to keep things running smoothly

Version 1.0.4

June 7, 2026
  • Added a total row to the contribution and withdrawal summary
  • Fixed transaction dates and yearly summaries shifting when the device changed time zones
  • Corrected URLs and improved menu bar items on Mac
  • Other bug fixes and optimizations

Version 1.0.3

May 7, 2026
  • Streamlined account reconciliation user interface
  • Added a more helpful 'Contact Us' link in settings
  • Other bug fixes and optimizations

Version 1.0.2

April 20, 2026
  • Redesigned onboarding video pages with a blur panel behind the title text for better legibility
  • Fixed provider autocomplete suggestions staying visible after tapping a suggestion
  • Fixed Terms of Service and other links in Settings not refreshing after remote updates
  • General polish and stability improvements

Version 1.0.1

February 25, 2026
  • New 'Siri Shortcuts' screen in Settings shows all the voice commands you can use with HSA Monster
  • Refreshed transaction detail, receipt cards, and Why HSA? resource cards
  • Fixed Siri shortcuts triggering the wrong action
  • Fixed 'Rate the App' button in Settings
  • General polish and stability improvements
