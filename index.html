Frequently Asked Questions

What is the HSA Triple Tax Advantage?

HSAs offer three tax benefits: contribute tax-free, invest for tax-free growth, and withdraw tax-free for qualified medical expenses. HSA Monster helps you track out-of-pocket expenses so you can maximize deferred reimbursements and let your investments grow.

Is my data secure?

Yes. All AI processing happens on your device so your receipts never leave your phone. No login to remember or server to depend on. Data syncs via iCloud with end-to-end encryption.

Can I export my receipts?

Yes! Export transactions, statements, or complete receipt archives as .zip files. Your data syncs across iPhone, iPad, and Mac, and you can export everything whenever you need it.

What if I want to switch to another app?

Export annual reports and full receipt archives any time. Move your data to another platform whenever you want. There's no lockin and your receipts will even be dated, named and organized.

Can my spouse add receipts too?

Absolutely! Share via iCloud and track expenses together. Everyone can add receipts, and changes sync instantly across all devices. With the annual plan, one license covers your entire Apple Family.

What devices are supported?

HSA Monster works on iPhone and iPad running iOS/iPadOS 18.6 or later, and Mac via Catalyst. For the best AI-powered receipt scanning, your device must support and have Apple Intelligence enabled.

Does HSA Monster require iCloud storage?

Yes, HSA Monster uses your personal iCloud to sync receipts and data across devices. There are no third-party servers so your data never leaves your device or your iCloud account. If you store many receipts, you may need to increase your iCloud storage plan.

Why does HSA Monster cost money?

HSA receipts need to be stored for decades. That's why HSA Monster avoids the typical model of storing your data on developer-managed servers behind a login. Instead, your receipts are stored in your own iCloud account using Apple's native technologies. By reducing dependence on our infrastructure, your records remain under your control. Your purchase supports continued development so we can keep improving the app for the long haul.
